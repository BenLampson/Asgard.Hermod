# Code of Conduct

We want Asgard Hermod to be a respectful, welcoming project for contributors of every background and experience level.

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Respect differing viewpoints and focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or personal attacks.
- Do not disclose another person's private information without permission.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other project community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation, with actions proportionate to the behavior.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner through a contact channel listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/BenLampson). Do not post private details in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and fairly.
